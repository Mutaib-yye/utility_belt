4 · Rules I will follow

Captions, cuts, graphics, sound

Captions

  • One word at a time, from Whisper word timings, placed at chest height (about 57% from the top).
  • Baloo 2 ExtraBold, white, soft dark shadow, no box.
  • Emphasis words go on a red pill: product names, prices, numbers, and the emotional word. About one in five words.
  • Pop in with a small scale-up over 4 frames. Keep the caption off the face.

Cuts, zooms, pacing

  • Remove silences longer than 0.25s. Aim for 220+ words per minute.
  • Something visible changes at least every 2.5s: a cut, a punch-in, a card or a sticker.
  • Alternate between wide and a 115% punch-in on cuts. This is my reading of the frames, so tell me if you want it gentler.
  • Hook inside the first 3 seconds. No logo intro.
  • Target length 20–45s. The references run 26–97s, but those are tutorials.

Motion graphics

  • Cards slide or spring in from the top third and leave within 3s.
  • Real product shots and real prices from aiidell.com on the cards. I will ask you to confirm prices before they go in a video.
  • Price tags and status labels use red pills, like the "WAITLIST" labels in reel 3.
  • Logo appears as a small chip on lower thirds and on the last frame, never as a watermark over the face.
  • Stickers and memes sparingly: at most two per video.

Sound

  • Finish at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak under −1 dB.
  • Voice first. My proposal, not measured from the reels: a soft music bed about 20 dB under the voice, and a short pop on each card arriving.
  • Music must be royalty-free or from your own library. I will not rip tracks from the reels.

5 · What we are marketing

AiiDell facts I will work from

The offer

Gifts for people in India, paid for in Indonesian rupiah. An India-based team buys locally and delivers, with no international parcels. Services include gifts and hampers, flowers, cakes, handwritten love letters, mobile recharge, order tracking and food delivery in about 40 minutes.

Who it is for

Indonesians sending gifts to a partner or family in India, especially long-distance couples, around birthdays, anniversaries, Eid, Diwali, Rakhi and Valentine's Day.

Voice

Warm, playful, reassuring, a little romantic. Lines from the site I may use: "Pick the person, we'll pick the gift", "Gifts that actually arrived", "Bought locally, delivered inside India".

Claims I will not use without you

"3,200+ happy customers" and "4.9 average rating" appear on the site, but I could not verify either. I will only use them if you confirm they are current.
